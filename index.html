        HOW TO PLAY

        • 4 rounds, 3 puzzles each. Every puzzle has its own clock: two birds walk the tightrope toward each other.
        • Just start typing the first three letters of an answer. Right? The rest fills in. Wrong? Your turn is over.
        • Type ahead: keep going while a word fills in. YELBLU solves YELLOW then BLUE.
        • Round 1: a clue and one scrambled word. Round 2: three answers in orange boxes, any order.
        • Round 3: a story with scrambled phrases. Round 4: a whole scrambled passage. Storybook words can be solved in any order.
        • If the birds meet, they crash and the answers are shown. Faster = more points. Esc pauses solo games.
        • Multiplayer: 2-6 players get the same puzzles at the same time. The server keeps the clock and the score.
